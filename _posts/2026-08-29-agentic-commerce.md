Customers are increasingly interacting with AI agents for buy products and services. We at our startup have deployed AI agents who are performing agent 
tasks for commerce. Hundreds of tasks are completed everyday. We are overwhelmed by the growth. Looking at this trends we expect that in few years 
majority of commerce will be done by agents.

We are seeing a massive demand for AI agents. In almost of every deployment we have made, customers are very happy with the results. The business are 
augmented with AI agents. This helps running business more efficiently and allows more room for growth.

As the agents grow in numbers there would probably be whole ecosystem for targeting AI agents. Businesses who have AI agents can integrate them like tools 
and services. I think there would be a massive opportunity for software and AI companies which would sustain the AI boom as well. Because the AI agents 
are surely capable and they would need additional tools and support.
